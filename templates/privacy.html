Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy explains what information Spendly collects when you track your expenses, why we collect it, and the choices you have over it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what the service needs in order to work:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data — a session cookie that keeps you signed in as you move between pages.

We do not connect to your bank, import transactions automatically, or ask for card or account numbers. Every expense in Spendly is one you typed in yourself.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Store your expenses and show them back to you.
  • Produce the category breakdowns and monthly summaries you request.
  • Keep the service working and diagnose technical problems.

We do not use your expense data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Your data is held in the application's database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed, so they cannot be read back, even by us.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you should keep your own records of anything you rely on.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

Your expenses are visible only to you when you are signed in. We do not share personal information with third parties except where we are required to by law, or where it is strictly necessary to operate the service.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses a single essential cookie to maintain your signed-in session. It contains no advertising or tracking identifiers, and it is cleared when you sign out. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You remain in control of your information. You can:

  • View and edit any expense you have recorded.
  • Delete individual expenses at any time.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

Deletion is permanent — once your account and records are removed, they cannot be recovered.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account and expense records for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, the associated data is removed from the live service.

8. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service develops. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date shown above. Continuing to use Spendly after a change means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or about the data we hold, please get in touch through the contact details listed on our website.
